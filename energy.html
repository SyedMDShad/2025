BATTERY CALIBRATION (manual)

Enter current voltage + load → app auto-sets battery % from the 12V rest-voltage curve (IR-compensated) and continues charge/discharge counting from that point. That voltage is also used for live battery amps for 20 min.

TUYA DAILY BASELINE (SmartLife "Today Ele")

Pick a date + enter what SmartLife shows for that day. Today's value feeds the live "max of all"; a past date backfills that day's Tuya entry. The app also auto-collects Tuya units itself every poll, so this is only needed to seed/correct a day.

METERING (BDT/kWh)

Default both 15. Reference: grid slot ≈13–16 h = 15 · solar-time & >600-unit slab = 17.35. Change a rate → every cost/saving in the app recalculates instantly.
